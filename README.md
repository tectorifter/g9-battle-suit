# g9-battle-suit
Index for all the mods related to gen 9 mods for g1r deluxe

Minimum required installations: national_dex, g9-battle-engine (gen9dex), battle_forms and g9-evolutions.

*national_dex by Sanjinpepic, registree of all pokemon up to gen9, moves, abilities and items.
https://github.com/sanjinpepic/gen1recomp-national-dex

*battle_forms by Sanjinpepic, adds ways to alternate forms for rotom + gimmmicks.
https://github.com/sanjinpepic/gen1recomp-battle-forms

*battle engine is the combat brain for modernized combat ruels.
https://github.com/tectorifter/Gen9Dex
*national_dex adds all the pokemon up to gen 9, move sets, combat items, learn sets, and abilities.

*g9-evolutions adds methods and alternate methods for all modern evolutions and alternate evolutions.
https://github.com/tectorifter/g9-evolutions

*g9-battle-sprites adds gen 5 style animated sprites for almost all pokemon until gen9.
https://github.com/tectorifter/g9-battle-sprites

*g9-gui a modernized gui for the whole game (gen 1 gen 2) compatible with modern stats
https://github.com/tectorifter/g9-gui

*g9-battle-scene, adds modernized combat gui, 2v2, 3v3, 4v1 and hordes battles.
https://github.com/tectorifter/g9-battle-scenes

*g9-battle-sample, adds randomizer, many qols compatible with battle engine, shortened pokecenter heal, and many more things.
https://github.com/tectorifter/g9-battle-sample

G9-evolutions explanation:
g9-evolutions will have the PC tool addition needed, will have a setting for on/off in case you don't want gimmicks.
